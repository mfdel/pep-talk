# Theme: `gratitude-winddown` — three good things and one thing you did

Shared rules: `themes/guide.md` (read first). Length ~6 min. Sleep only.

## Intent

The plain nightly rep. No goal, no critic work, no event. Three good moments from today, each with
one sense and the listener's own part in it, then one action they took today, counted on the action
scoreboard whatever the response. Then the day is let go. It is the default for an ordinary night
and for nights when the mind keeps replaying the inbox, the feed or the to-do list.

- **Best for:** ordinary nights; rumination before sleep; noisy days with nothing in particular to
  process; no topic given.
- **Not for:** mornings; a fresh rejection or a harsh day (use `compassion`); a day with a visible
  act such as a talk, an interview, a post or a hard conversation (use `debrief`); goals or events.

## Evidence basis

| Element | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Three good things, with why they happened | Daily "three good things" raised well-being for months in a randomised trial | Seligman, Steen, Park & Peterson 2005 |
| Gratitude at bedtime | Gratitude predicted better sleep via more positive, fewer negative pre-sleep thoughts | Wood, Joseph, Lloyd & Atkins 2009 |
| Short gratitude list before bed | Small improvement in pre-sleep worry and sleep quality (pilot trial: modest evidence) | Digdon & Koble 2011 |

## Inputs

Best: the user's prompt names today's moments. If it does not, **do not invent a day.** Switch to
invite mode: each "good thing" becomes a prompt to find one ("Find one moment today when…") followed
by a silence of 12–15 s. Invite mode always has a floor, so a flat day cannot turn into evidence
against the listener: "If nothing comes, the bed under you counts." The one action for section 3
can come from the brief (§5); if nothing is dated today, invite it the same way.

## Structure

Pace ≈ 130 wpm. **430–470 spoken words** (≈ 3.5 min) + **≈ 150 s of `[[PAUSE n]]`** ≈ 6 min.
Characters 2,700–3,300. Invite mode: fewer words (≈ 380), more silence (≈ 190 s).

| # | Section | Words | Pause budget | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrive | 45–70 | ~20 s | Name, breaths with one `[sighing]`. The day is done; nothing more is asked of it |
| 2 | Three good things | 180–210 | ~55 s | Each: what happened, one sense, and their part in it ("you made that happen by…"). Small is fine: a coffee, a laugh at home, a bug fixed |
| 3 | One thing you did | 60–80 | ~20 s | One action from today, banked on the action scoreboard regardless of the reply. The silence where the listener says "I did that." themselves |
| 4 | Let the day go | 80–100 | ~45 s | Softer, slower. `[whispering]`, then `[sleepy drowsy voice]` for the last chunk. End on `[[PAUSE 30]]` |

## Endings

Sleep only. No count-up, no plan, no tomorrow list. If the user asks for a wake version, route to
`evidence-reel` or `custom` instead.

## Brief must contain

- §1 routing (a visible act today moves the theme to `debrief`; a raw day to `compassion`).
- §5: anything dated today — small good moments with their sense, and one act for section 3.
- §9 their own lines, if one fits the close; §14 never mention.
- **Fallback:** invite mode.

The brief agent finds this best in today's journal entry or daily note, a wins log, and the prompt.

## Affirmations

None. The ownership line "I did that." is a fixed part of this theme, spoken in section 3, so
nothing new goes to `.pep-talk/affirmations.md`. Skip the log.

## Worked example — section 2 (excerpt), invite mode

```
# 2. Three good things
[softly] Alex… find one moment today that went well. Small is fine.
[[PAUSE 12]]
[slowly] Stay there for a breath. What did you see? What did you hear?
[[PAUSE 6]]
[softly] Now notice your part in it. Something you did, or chose, or noticed.
It counts.
[[PAUSE 6]]
[slowly] Find a second one. A word with someone. A quiet minute. Anything.
[[PAUSE 12]]
[softly] Let it be as simple as it was. It does not need to be big to be real.
[[PAUSE 6]]
[slowly] And a third. If nothing comes, the bed under you counts.
[[PAUSE 12]]
[softly] Three good things. All of them happened. All of them are yours to keep, Alex.
Let them be enough for tonight.
[[PAUSE 8]]
```

## Don'ts

- No goals, no plans, no "tomorrow you will". No critic work; if the critic is loud, use
  `compassion`.
- No invented events, people or moments. Invite mode beats fiction.
- No grading the day ("a productive day"). Good things are not achievements.
- No wake-only tags; no scene cues except one `[bell ringing]` at the very start as a settling cue.
